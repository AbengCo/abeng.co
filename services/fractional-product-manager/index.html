Questions

What is a fractional product manager?

A senior product manager who works with your team part-time, usually for a few months, instead of a full-time hire.

How much does a fractional product manager cost?

It depends on the days per week and the length of the engagement. We price it as a monthly fee or an hourly rate, in writing before we start.

How is this different from a contractor?

A fractional product manager owns product decisions and process, not just tasks, and works with your leadership team.

Do you work with teams outside Canada?

Yes. We work fully remotely with teams in Canada, the US and other time zones.
