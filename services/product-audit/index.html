Product audit for consumer apps and fintech

A short, fixed-scope review of your product, roadmap, data and funnel. You get a clear, prioritized list of what to fix and build next, and the reasons behind it.

What you get

  • A walkthrough of your product as a new user would see it, with friction points marked
  • A review of your roadmap and backlog against your business goals
  • A check of your analytics and funnel: what is tracked, what is missing, and where users drop off
  • A written report with a prioritized list of fixes and opportunities, sized by effort and impact
  • A 60-minute review call to go through the findings with your team

Who it's for

  • Consumer apps and fintech products that are live and have users
  • Founders who feel the roadmap is driven by whoever spoke last
  • Teams about to raise, relaunch or hire their first product manager
  • Leaders who want an outside, senior view before a big decision

How it works

  1. 1. Intro callA free 20-minute call to confirm the audit is the right fit.
  2. 2. Access and interviewsWe get access to your product and analytics, and talk to 2 to 4 people on your team.
  3. 3. Report and reviewYou get the written report and a review call. The findings are yours to keep.

Questions

How much does a product audit cost?

From US$3,500 as a fixed fee. Larger products or extra interviews may cost more, and you get the price in writing before anything starts. Canadian clients can be invoiced in Canadian dollars.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks from kickoff, depending on how quickly we get access to your product and team.

What do you need from us?

Access to the product and your analytics tool, your current roadmap, and an hour or two with a few people on your team.

What happens after the audit?

Many teams act on the report themselves. If you want help with delivery, we can continue as a fractional product lead.
