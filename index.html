What clients say

Questions

What does a product audit include?

A review of your product, roadmap, data and team process, then a written, prioritized list of what to fix and build next. It's short and fixed in scope.

What is a fractional product manager?

A senior product manager who works with your team part-time, for example to cover a gap between full-time product managers or to get a launch over the line.

Do you help with launches and marketing?

Yes, on the product side: go-to-market plans, launch strategy, funnels and conversion analytics. For hands-on ad campaigns we bring in trusted specialists.

Which companies do you work with?

Mainly consumer apps (B2C) and fintech companies, both B2C and B2B, from startups to established teams.

What can you automate?

Repeat operations work: reports, data entry, hand-offs between tools and spreadsheet steps, using automation and practical AI tools.

How much does it cost?

A product audit starts from US$3,500 and takes about 2 weeks. Other work is priced as a fixed project fee or an hourly rate, depending on the work. You get the pricing in writing before anything starts. Canadian clients can be invoiced in Canadian dollars.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, fully remotely. We're based in Toronto and work across time zones.
